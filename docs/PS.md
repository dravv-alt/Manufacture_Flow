Executive Summary Manufacturing plants consist of multiple workstations through which production jobs pass in a predefined sequence. Any unexpected workstation breakdown can result in production delays, missed shipment commitments, resource underutilization, and increased operational costs. The objective of this solution is to implement an **AI-driven Predictive Maintenance and Intelligent Production Re-Routing System** that can proactively detect potential workstation failures before they occur and automatically initiate corrective actions across maintenance, warehouse, procurement, production planning, and logistics teams. The system shall continuously monitor workstation health through sensors, operational parameters, machine logs, and historical maintenance records to predict component failures. Once a potential failure is detected, the system shall coordinate actions across various departments without requiring manual intervention. ### 2. Business Problem Statement Currently, workstation failures are often detected only after a breakdown occurs. This results in: * Production stoppages * Order delivery delays * Unplanned downtime * Manual coordination between departments * Inventory shortages * Increased maintenance costs * Customer dissatisfaction The organization requires a proactive mechanism that can: * Predict machine failures before they occur * Check spare part availability automatically * Procure missing components * Re-route production jobs * Inform stakeholders in real time * Predict machine recovery timelines ### 3.
